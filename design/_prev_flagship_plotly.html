Why it matters

The problem

Subsurface data is scarce

Depth-resolved ocean measurements rely on sparse, costly in-situ floats. The surface, though, is imaged daily by satellites.

Our approach

Learn surface → depth

A CNN encoder–decoder maps six surface fields (SST, SSH, SSS, winds, currents) into 15 depth levels, trained on GLORYS reanalysis.

The result

Skill at every depth

0.87 °C RMSE against real ARGO floats — beating the baseline at 14 of 15 depth levels, and within 0.14 °C of the GLORYS reanalysis ceiling.

Honest positioning: the surface→subsurface approach exists in the literature. Our contribution is adapting it to the Indian Ocean, with strict leak-free validation against real ARGO floats and an end-to-end, deployable pipeline.
