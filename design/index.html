OceanEmbed AI Report

Comprehensive overview of subsurface ocean temperature predictions and validation metrics.

1. AI Validation & Accuracy

What this is: This graph shows how accurate our AI is compared to real ocean sensors (ARGO floats) at different depths. Lower numbers mean the AI's prediction is very close to reality.

2. Sensor Matchup (Float Explorer)

What this is: This compares the AI's predicted temperatures against actual underwater sensor readings. Points tightly hugging the diagonal line mean extremely high accuracy.

3. Surface Domain Map

What this is: This is a top-down map of the ocean surface temperatures across the entire region, showing exactly where the water is warm (red) or cold (blue).

4. 3D Ocean Volume

What this is: This is a full 3D interactive model of the ocean generated by the AI. It allows us to look deep underwater to see hidden temperature layers beneath the surface.

5. Depth Transect (Cross-Section)

What this is: This is a vertical slice of the ocean (like cutting a cake). It clearly shows how the temperature drops as you go deeper underwater along a specific latitude or longitude line.

6. Ocean State (Thermocline & Mixed-Layer)

What this is: This map calculates the exact depth where the water suddenly gets very cold (the Thermocline). This is highly useful for submarines, as sound waves bounce off this cold layer, hiding them from sonar.
